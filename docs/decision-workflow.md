# Review, concurrence, and approval

An action assignment can carry one of four purposes: ordinary action, review, concurrence, or formal
approval. Decision requests are separate audit records linked to the recipient's work item.

- Review permits **Recommend** or **Return to requester**.
- Concurrence permits **Concur**, **Reject**, or **Return to requester**.
- Approval permits **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Return to requester**.
- A decision request has exactly one accountable recipient; other interested staff may be copied.
- Decision notes are mandatory and the actor and timestamp are recorded.
- A pending decision blocks forwarding and resolution by that recipient.
- A positive decision keeps custody with the decision-maker, who can then forward or resolve the matter.
- Rejection or return completes the decision work item and creates a new action item for the requester.

This supports Director ICT requesting formal approval from Director Human Resources. After approving,
Director Human Resources can route the correspondence upward to the DG. The decision remains visible in
the decision register and immutable correspondence timeline after later movements.
